# Fontes das animações V1

Geradas com a skill imagegen a partir dos personagens raster V2 aprovados. PNGs originais preservados em `public/arte/animacoes-fontes/`; o navegador baixa somente a folha WebP da raça em uso.

Cada folha tem 4×4 quadros: repouso (inclui piscar), caminhada no lugar, ataque corporal genérico e gesto de trabalho. Ferramentas por profissão, armas equipadas e reações de dano ainda não têm sequências próprias. A animação é ilustrativa; não confirma golpes ou resultados. O motor/save não foi alterado.

Bake reproduzível: `node scripts/preparar-animacoes.mjs humano anao elfo orc lobisomem sangue-palido draconato silfo`. Usa alpha, maior componente, pivô de apoio e escala uniforme por folha. Saída lossless 1024×1024, 16 células de 256px, metadados de limites por quadro. A paleta é aplicada no cliente, por linha, preservando os materiais de pele/couro/metal. Retratos V2 permanecem estáticos.

Playback tem cadência por sequência, pausa em leitura/aba oculta/movimento reduzido e ritmo mais lento no Limiar. Falha ou carregamento usa o corpo V2 detalhado; reconectar tenta novamente. Caches LRU: quatro folhas e oito linhas personalizadas. Service worker guarda somente assets visitados.

## Prompts exatos

### humano

Referências: personagens-v2/humano.png.

```text
Use the attached approved HUMAN character as identity and art style reference. Create ONE production animation sprite sheet for this EXACT character, no redesign. Transparent PNG, square canvas, EXACT 4 columns x 4 rows, 16 equal square cells, one full-body sprite per cell, no text/labels/grid/borders. Same face, silhouette, haircut, boots, leather belt, gold buckle and detailed crisp pixel-art clusters as reference, front three-quarter facing RIGHT in every cell. Character must fit in each cell with ample transparent margins even with an extended arm. Keep head size, proportions, camera, pixel size and foot baseline perfectly consistent. Feet fully visible, do not overlap cells. MATERIAL COLORS for runtime palette swap: head hair and eyebrows use shaded MAGENTA around hue 300 (#bf30bf midtone, deep purple-magenta shadows); main tunic uses shaded CYAN around hue 185 (#20b8c8). Keep skin tan, eyes brown, leather brown, metal gold, white cuffs/trim ivory. Do not use marker colors on skin/eyes/boots. ROW 1 = IDLE loop: neutral relaxed eyes open; gentle inhale with shoulders subtly raised; blink eyes closed with relaxed stance; exhale eyes open returning to neutral. ROW 2 = WALK-IN-PLACE loop: left foot forward and right arm forward; passing stance with feet nearly together; right foot forward and left arm forward; opposite passing stance. Clearly articulate different legs and arms, no translation of a frozen sprite. ROW 3 = UNARMED ATTACK: balanced ready guard with bent knees; windup drawing right fist back and rotating torso slightly; clear rightward punch with arm extended and forward weight; recovery pulling fist back toward ready stance. No weapons, no energy streaks, no effects or enemies. ROW 4 = WORK GESTURE loop: ready hands at waist; lean forward reaching both hands out; pull hands toward chest with elbows bent and slight torso effort; lower hands returning to ready. No tools or invented props. Anatomically convincing hands and limbs, not rigid block figures. All four frames in each row should make a usable looping sequence. Preserve premium friendly-serious medieval RPG pixel-art quality, no painterly blur, no antialiasing halos, genuinely transparent background.
```
